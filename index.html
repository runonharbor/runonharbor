Built while growing a real business.

Growing the business
wasn't the hard part. Keeping everything organized was.

We didn't set out to build software. We were just trying to keep up with a growing business.

As CSC grew, we found ourselves constantly solving the same problems over and over again.

We were searching inboxes for the latest supplier pricing, digging through email threads to find customer conversations, trying to remember whether someone had followed up on a quote, and maintaining spreadsheets simply to keep track of what was happening.

Every time we encountered a new pain point, we built a better way to handle it.

Over time, those solutions started connecting together.

What began as a way to keep track of customers and suppliers evolved into a place where we could manage quotes, pricing, requests, assets, documents, follow-ups, and workflows without constantly jumping between systems.


Read Our Story

It worked. Until it didn't.

Email worked. Spreadsheets worked. Shared folders worked. Sticky notes worked.

Then the business grew.

Nobody knew where the latest pricing was. Follow-ups started slipping through the cracks. Customer history became difficult to find. Important details lived everywhere.

None of the tools were necessarily broken. We were simply asking each of them to do more than they were designed to do.
