Harbor
Built while growing a real business.

Growing the business
wasn't the hard part.

Keeping everything organized was.

As CSC grew, we found ourselves constantly solving the same problems over and over again.

We were searching inboxes for the latest supplier pricing, digging through email threads to find customer conversations, trying to remember whether someone had followed up on a quote, and maintaining spreadsheets simply to keep track of what was happening.

Every time we encountered a new pain point, we built a better way to handle it.

Over time, those solutions started connecting together.

What began as a way to keep track of customers and suppliers evolved into a place where we could manage quotes, pricing, requests, assets, documents, follow-ups, and workflows without constantly jumping between systems.

Today, we can run much of our business without searching through inboxes for the latest quote, digging through spreadsheets for information, or wondering whether something slipped through the cracks.

Harbor wasn't built from a business plan.
It was built while building a business.
The messy middle

Growth creates a different kind of problem.

At first, email, spreadsheets, shared folders, and memory are enough.

Then the business starts moving faster.

More customers. More suppliers. More quotes. More pricing updates. More follow-ups. More people needing the latest answer.

That is when the tools that helped you get started begin creating friction.

What changed

We stopped trying to make the inbox run the business.

Your inbox is still great for communication.

But it was never meant to be your CRM, quote tracker, supplier database, pricing history, document library, and follow-up system.

Harbor gives the important parts of the business somewhere better to live.

What Harbor organizes

One place for the things that usually get scattered.

Customers & conversations

Keep customer history, requests, quotes, notes, and follow-ups connected instead of buried across inboxes.

Suppliers & pricing

Track supplier details, pricing updates, documents, and sourcing conversations in one reliable place.

Assets & requests

Connect equipment, parts, service needs, requests, and workflows so your team knows where things stand.

Built from experience

We built this with a small team while managing thousands of items.

Harbor was not built because we wanted to launch another software product.

It was built because we needed a better way to manage a real business with a small team, a growing catalog, supplier relationships, customer requests, quote activity, and operational details that could not keep living in scattered places.

That is the part we care about most: helping small teams operate with more clarity without adding unnecessary complexity.

Harbor is for businesses that have outgrown the way they started, but do not want to become buried in software just to stay organized.
