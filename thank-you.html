Thank you for taking the time to check out Harbor and learn more about what we're building.

If you're here, there's a good chance you've felt the same frustration I did while growing my business: important customer conversations, supplier communication, quotes, orders, and key decisions scattered across emails, spreadsheets, and disconnected systems.

Harbor was born from that experience. We're building a platform that helps businesses keep important information in one place, streamline supplier and customer communication, and spend less time digging through email threads looking for the details needed to move work forward.

We're still early in our journey, but I'm excited about what we're building and grateful that you're taking a look. I'll personally follow up soon to learn more about your business and hear what challenges you're facing.
